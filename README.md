# Product Research, Market Analysis & Pricing Strategy

This portfolio project presents a structured product research and data analysis workflow for marketplace businesses, e-commerce sellers, and online brands.

## Project Overview

I conducted structured research on products, pricing, markets, and competitors to support a better listing strategy, pricing decisions, and marketplace growth planning.

The project focused on competitor benchmarking, price band mapping, demand-gap identification, and margin-aware recommendations. The final insights were delivered through decision-ready spreadsheets and summary reports to help businesses take faster and more practical action.
